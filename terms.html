1. Website Build Packages & Scope of Work

By purchasing a website build package, media package, or subscription from 624 Siteworks ("Agency", "we", "us"), the purchasing business or individual ("Client", "you") agrees to the scope boundaries defined below:

  • Starter Build ($595 One-Time): Includes a complete 4-page custom static website (Home page + up to 3 additional pages such as Services, About, and Contact), mobile optimization, click-to-call integration, lead capture form, and foundational local SEO setup.
  • Pro Build ($995 One-Time): Includes up to 7 pages total (Home page + up to 6 additional pages for dedicated services or galleries), advanced local JSON-LD schema markup, and Google PageSpeed / Lighthouse 95+ performance tuning.
  • Premium Build ($1,695 One-Time — $2,024+ Bundled Value): Includes up to 10 custom pages, Google Business Profile setup/optimization, the On-Site Photo, Video & Drone Package ($495 value) within 60 miles of Appleton, WI, and six (6) months of the Peace of Mind Plan ($534 value) included at no additional charge.
  • Stand-Alone / Add-On Photo, Video & Drone Package ($495 One-Time): Can be added to a Starter ($595 + $495 = $1,090) or Pro ($995 + $495 = $1,490) website build, or purchased as a stand-alone shoot for an existing website or marketing campaign.
  • Additional Pages ($150/Page): Any pages requested beyond the page limit of your selected tier are billed at a flat rate of $150 per additional page.

Baseline Architecture Lock: When claiming a live concept preview, your build fee covers customizing the branding, color palette, typography, service text, contact details, and photography within the established layout structure. It does not include a ground-up custom software rebuild or a complete structural redesign after kickoff.

2. Pre-Launch Revisions & Sign-Off Policy

Following purchase, we conduct a 1-on-1 kickoff call to gather your exact wording adjustments, service details, and photo selections.

  • Two (2) Consolidated Revision Rounds: Every website build includes up to two (2) rounds of consolidated revisions prior to launch. A "revision round" means a single consolidated list of adjustments provided by the Client after reviewing the updated staging site.
  • Post-Approval or Extra Revisions: Once the second revision round is completed—or once the Client approves the site for live domain deployment—the initial build scope is complete. Any additional pre-launch revision rounds or structural changes requested thereafter will be billed at our standard hourly rate of $125/hour (or applied toward your monthly update credits if subscribed to the Peace of Mind Plan).

3. Client Content & 14-Day Launch Window

We pride ourselves on launching websites quickly so you can start generating leads immediately.

  • 14-Day Asset Turnaround: Client agrees to provide any requested logos, custom job-site photos, license/insurance numbers, or specific text edits within 14 calendar days of the kickoff call.
  • Authorization to Launch with Trade-Verified Assets: If custom photos or text are not received within 14 days, 624 Siteworks is authorized to finalize and launch the website using our trade-pure commercial photography and professionally written contractor copywriting so your launch is not delayed. If you are enrolled in the Peace of Mind Plan, your custom photos can be swapped in later as part of your regular update credits.

4. "Peace of Mind" Hosting & Care Plan ($89/mo or $890/yr)

The optional (and recommended) Peace of Mind Plan handles your technical infrastructure and ongoing website upkeep for a predictable flat rate:

  • Billing Options: Clients may choose $89/month (billed monthly) or $890/year (billed annually, reflecting a "2 Months Free" discount of $178/year).
  • What is Included: Enterprise global CDN hosting, SSL security certificate management, domain DNS management, form routing maintenance, and up to two (2) consolidated content update credits per month (up to 6 per calendar quarter).
  • Definition of a Consolidated Content Update Credit: Each update credit covers up to 30 minutes of work submitted in a single consolidated email or text message. Eligible updates include swapping project gallery photos/videos, updating phone numbers or business hours, adjusting service descriptions or pricing text, adding customer reviews/testimonials, or posting a seasonal promotional banner on an existing page.
  • Quarterly Rollover & Strict Calendar Quarter Reset: Unused monthly update credits automatically roll over from month to month within the current calendar quarter, up to a maximum of 6 consolidated edits per quarter (for monthly subscribers, 2 credits accrue upon each paid monthly billing cycle). Calendar quarters are strictly locked to:
    • Q1: January 1 – March 31
    • Q2: April 1 – June 30
    • Q3: July 1 – September 30
    • Q4: October 1 – December 31
    At 11:59 PM on the final day of each calendar quarter (March 31, June 30, September 30, and December 31), any unused update credits expire and do not roll over into the next quarter.
  • Requests Exceeding Quarterly Limits: If an update request requires more than 30 minutes of work, or if you have exhausted your credits for the current quarter, we will notify you upfront and bill the additional time at $125/hour.

5. Static Architecture & Out-of-Scope Exclusions

624 Siteworks engineers custom high-speed static websites designed to achieve 95+ Google PageSpeed scores and maximum security. Unless explicitly quoted in a separate written agreement, our packages and care plans do not include:

  • WordPress or third-party CMS backend editing dashboards or client login portals.
  • E-commerce shopping carts, inventory databases, or custom interactive software applications.
  • Third-party CRM, ERP, or scheduling software API integrations (beyond standard webhook/email lead form delivery).
  • Full custom vector logo design or print branding packages (we format and optimize your existing logo or create a clean typographic brand header).
  • Ongoing paid advertising management (Google Ads / Meta Ads PPC management) or third-party directory fees.
  • Hourly Rate for Approved Additions: Any out-of-scope custom development or design work approved by the Client is billed at $125/hour (1-hour minimum).

6. Build-Only (Self-Managed) Projects

If you elect to purchase a one-time website build without the Peace of Mind Plan:

  • Hand-Off & 14-Day Warranty: We will deploy the site to your hosting account (or provide a complete ZIP archive of all production HTML/CSS/JS/image files) and provide a 14-day post-launch warranty window to fix any technical bugs present at launch.
  • Client Responsibility After 14 Days: After the 14-day window, the Client is solely responsible for hosting costs, SSL renewals, domain DNS configuration, form endpoints, and content edits. Any future edits, updates, or technical support requested by a Build-Only client are billed at $125/hour (1-hour minimum).

7. On-Site Photo, Video & Drone Package ($495 Stand-Alone / Included in Premium)

For clients purchasing the Premium Build ($1,695) or the On-Site Photo, Video & Drone Package ($495 Add-On or Stand-Alone):

  • Service Area (60-Mile Radius): On-site photography, video clips, and drone capture are included for locations within a 60-mile radius of Appleton, WI. Locations beyond 60 miles are subject to a flat travel surcharge agreed upon prior to scheduling.
  • Session Scope: Includes one (1) on-site shoot lasting up to 2 hours at a single shop or active job site location, delivering 15–25 professionally edited high-resolution photos plus ambient B-roll video clips for website headers/social media, with full commercial usage rights for the Client.
  • FAA & Weather Compliance: Aerial drone photography and video are performed by an FAA-compliant operator and are strictly subject to FAA airspace restrictions, weather conditions (wind/precipitation), and job-site safety. If weather or airspace prohibits drone flight, additional ground-level architectural and team media will be captured, or the aerial portion will be rescheduled once at no charge.

  • Build Fee Refund Policy: Because 624 Siteworks invests upfront engineering time to create a live preview of your website before you pay—and immediately allocates customization and domain setup resources upon purchase—one-time build fees ($595 / $995 / $1,695) and photography add-ons ($495) are non-refundable once the kickoff or customization process begins.
  • Monthly Plan Cancellation: The $89/mo Peace of Mind Plan is month-to-month with zero cancellation fees. You may cancel at any time by emailing hello@624siteworks.com or calling (920) 234-5633 at least 3 business days before your next billing cycle. Upon cancellation, we will provide a ZIP archive of your static site files or assist with pointing your domain to your new host.

You may cancel auto-renewal at any time prior to your annual renewal date, and your hosting and monthly update benefits will remain active through the end of your paid 12-month term.
  • Portfolio Right: Unless requested otherwise in writing, 624 Siteworks reserves the right to display screenshots and links to the completed website in our agency portfolio and marketing materials.
