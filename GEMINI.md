# 624 SITEWORKS - PROJECT GUIDELINES & AGENT RULES

## 1. Absolute Image Context & Trade Relevance (STRICT ENFORCEMENT)

Every website build, automated lead engine run, and client pitch MUST maintain 100% fidelity between imagery and content:

1. **Trade Purity**:
   - Every single image must accurately and unmistakably depict the business's specific trade and industry.
   - NEVER cross-pollinate trades:
     - DO NOT place concrete work next to plumbing text.
     - DO NOT place plumbing pipes next to roofing text.
     - DO NOT place generic indoor stock photos on exterior contractor sites.

2. **Sub-Trade & Material Contextual Alignment**:
   - Every image placed alongside text (e.g. service cards, feature highlights) must specifically match the exact materials, scope, and services described:
     - **Custom Wood / Cedar Fencing** -> Authentic Western Red Cedar, timber, or privacy wood fence imagery ONLY. NEVER vinyl, NEVER chain link.
     - **Vinyl / PVC Fencing** -> High-grade white vinyl or composite privacy fence imagery ONLY. NEVER wood, NEVER chain link.
     - **Chain Link & Security Fencing** -> Black vinyl-coated or galvanized chain link fence imagery ONLY. NEVER wood, NEVER vinyl.
     - **Ornamental Iron & Metal Gates** -> Wrought iron or black architectural aluminum gates ONLY.
     - **Shingle Roofing** -> Architectural asphalt shingle roofing ONLY. NEVER flat rubber or commercial gravel.
     - **Metal Roofing** -> Standing seam or architectural metal roofing ONLY.
     - **Flat / Commercial Roofing** -> TPO / EPDM membrane flat commercial roof ONLY.
     - **Stamped Concrete** -> Stamped, stained, or textured decorative concrete flatwork ONLY.
     - **Poured Driveways / Flatwork** -> Clean broomed or finished poured concrete driveway ONLY.
     - **Plumbing Fixtures vs Water Heaters vs Drain Lines** -> Show fixtures for fixture cards, water heaters for heater cards, and clean copper/PEX piping for pipework cards.
     - **Tree Trimming vs Stump Grinding vs Tree Removal** -> Show canopy pruning for trimming cards, stump grinding for stump cards, and crane/chainsaw felling for removal cards.

3. **Scraped Image Discipline**:
   - Only place client-scraped images into specific service slots if their source filename, alt text, or context explicitly confirms that material/service.
   - If an authentic scraped photo's material cannot be definitively verified, route it to `hero.jpg`, `about.jpg`, or general `gallery`, and use the contextually matched trade library for specific service cards.

4. **Dynamic Markup & Copywriting**:
   - All section headers, comparison tables, and testimonials must dynamically reflect the detected trade (e.g. `Roofing`, `Concrete`, `Plumbing`), never leaving hardcoded references to other trades.
